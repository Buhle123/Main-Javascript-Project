Read before playing

Open the browser console before you start.
It’s a hidden panel built into every desktop browser. Nothing to install.
Why?
The console keeps the full story log: every room you enter and everything you find, so you can scroll back and see what happened.
Where does everything appear?
Pop-up boxes show where you are and what you can do. The console, next to this page, keeps the whole story.
2

Open the console

Press these keys together while this page is open. The console opens at the side or bottom of the window.

BrowserWindows / LinuxMac
Chrome, Edge Ctrl+Shift+J Cmd+Option+J
Firefox Ctrl+Shift+K Cmd+Option+K
Safari Not available Cmd+Option+C
  • Any browser on Windows: F12, then click the Console tab. On some laptops you hold Fn as well.
  • Safari, first time only: open Safari Settings, go to Advanced and tick “Show features for web developers”.
  • On a phone or tablet? There’s no console, but you can still play: the pop-ups show everything you need.

How to play

  1. Open the console with the keys on this page.
  2. Press Start the game. Each pop-up tells you which room you’re in, what you’re carrying and what you can do.
  3. Type one of the choices it lists, like inspect or control room, and press OK. Capitals and extra spaces don’t matter.
  4. Typed something it doesn’t know? Nothing changes, and it shows you the choices again.
  5. Your choices decide the ending. Press Cancel in a typing box to quit safely.
